# Historical task dispatch measurements

`task-dispatch-results.json` preserves the measurement from the task dispatch PR branch.
`task-dispatch-main-results.json` preserves the separate measurement previously on main at
`a60693d3ce7df16429d2985b14a3d2aa6f2dedc9`, including its executor safety probe.

These are historical observations of the source fingerprint recorded in each file, not
validation results for the merged cancellation fixes. The runs are kept separately so
that resolving their merge conflict does not combine measurements from different runs.
